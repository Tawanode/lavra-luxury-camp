Welcome to
Lavra Luxury Camp

Positioned directly within the spectacular mountains of the Wadi Rum Protected Area, Lavra Luxury Camp is a 5-star aparthotel that balances rugged desert exploration with modern, upscale comfort. Located just a 5-minute walk from the heart of Wadi Rum and steps from the Visitor Center, our 30 distinct guestrooms and ground-floor apartments offer direct, unobstructed views of the Martian landscape.

Whether you are relaxing by our central outdoor fireplace under the stars, enjoying our multi-cuisine restaurant, or embarking on a guided jeep tour, our multilingual staff is dedicated to providing an unforgettable Jordanian adventure.

Sound-Insulated Comfort in Wadi Rum

How our thoughtful architecture provides restful sleep under clear starry skies.

Positioned directly within the dramatic mountains of the Wadi Rum Protected Area on Ram Street, Lavra Luxury Camp offers serene, restorative rest far from urban clamor.

All 30 guestrooms and ground-floor apartments feature sound-insulated walls and whisper-quiet climate-controlled air conditioning and heating. After an exhilarating day traversing red dunes and ancient canyons, retreat into a private sanctuary with deep bathtubs, plush bathrobes, and unobstructed mountain vistas.

Commitment to 5-Star Cleanliness & Comfort:Rooms feature climate-controlled AC and heating, sound-insulated walls, premium bedding with nightly turndown service, in-room safes, and ensuite bathrooms with walk-in showers or deep bathtubs.
